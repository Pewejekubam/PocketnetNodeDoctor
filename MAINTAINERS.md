## Scope

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, cut releases, and triage security
reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Decisions

Changes to the public CLI surface, the `plan.json` format, or the exit-code
contract are treated as compatibility-affecting and require maintainer sign-off.
